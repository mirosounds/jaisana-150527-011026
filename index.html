Music for Jai & Sana’s wedding weekend in Istanbul: a hybrid DJ interlude to launch the party in the Binbirdirek Cistern, and live R&B and soulful grooves for sunset cocktails overlooking the Bosphorus at the Sait Halim Pasha Mansion.

Artists

  • Fees: quoted all in, and outside the scope of UK VAT (no VAT added) - covering performance fees, flights, UK airport travel, per diems, curation, booking and on-site management across both days. Accommodation, local transfers and technical production are provided by the client (see Booking, Expenses & Logistics below).
  • Set & Format: Sophie’s sunset set is a bespoke 60-90 minute performance, rehearsed around your playlist, as a trio or quartet with the option of adding a male singer/rapper. Val’s DJ interlude runs around 60 minutes before handing over to your DJ.
  • Repertoire: Artists have extensive repertoires, and can work to a brief. The Saturday performance is based around your playlist.
  • Styling: Artists can be styled to your taste and to complement the event aesthetic and dress code.

Let us know which Saturday option you’d like, and we’ll confirm both performances with a 50% deposit.
Fees and holds are valid for 15 days.

Booking, Expenses & Logistics
  • All-in fees: Quoted fees include performance fees (including rehearsals for bespoke performances), flights, UK airport travel, per diems, booking and on-site management by Miro Sounds across both days.
  • Travel: Economy flights, avoiding departures before c.8am. Artists arrive the day before their performance, so there’s no risk from travel delays.
  • Provided by client: Accommodation - 5 individual rooms (6 for a quartet) covering the band, Val and an on-site manager: band 2 nights (Fri & Sat), Val 2 nights (Thu & Fri), on-site manager 3 nights (Thu-Sat). Also local ground transfers between airport, accommodation and venues; green room and show-day meal, and full technical production via the client’s production team - sound, lighting, staging, backline and instruments, DJ equipment and a dedicated sound engineer. We’ll liaise with them directly.
  • Changes & additional costs: Fees are based on the line-up, schedule and logistics set out here. Any changes after booking (e.g. timings, additional travel, line-up, expenses and costs) will be recharged.
  • Payment: 50% deposit to confirm each booking, with the balance due 30 days before the event.
  • Validity: Fees, availability holds and estimates are valid for 15 days unless otherwise agreed.

Booking Agreement: a full agreement will be shared, along with the deposit invoice, upon booking confirmation.
